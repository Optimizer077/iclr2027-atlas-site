01 / DATA

The route to the Atlas

Public submissions enter once; each stage adds another way to read them.

Reading the scores. Mean rating on the 1–4 scale, with review count and standard deviation. Papers are ordered by mean score; the confidence-weighted mean breaks ties. Withdrawn and desk-rejected papers remain in the index but are not ordered. No acceptance labels are shown — decisions are not ours.
02 / RETRIEVAL

Three ways to find a paper

The page checks availability in this order. The live demo uses the first available engine.

Checking the search engines…

01 · SERVERChecking

GPU semantic search

api/search embeds the query with bge-base on the RTX GPU, compares paper vectors, then adds a small lexical signal.

When it runsOnly when api/health reports ready. Around 15 ms is a reference latency; the demo measures this request.

02 · ON DEVICEChecking

Browser semantic search

EmbeddingGemma turns the query into a vector on your device. A dot product against the int8 document vectors selects the top-k papers.

When it runsWhen browser semantic search is enabled and its model and vectors are available. Enable it on Search ↗

03 · LOCAL INDEXReady

Keyword search

BM25-style term weights reward rare words and title matches across titles, keywords, TL;DRs, and categories. No model download.

When it runsThe fallback whenever neither semantic engine can run. Exact technical terms and paper titles work well here.
