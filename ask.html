On-device research assistant

Ask Gemma

Query or summarize ICLR 2027 submissions directly in your browser using Google’s Gemma 4 E2B (LiteRT / WebGPU), grounded strictly in Atlas papers, fact sheets, and abstracts.

Runs on your deviceEverything runs locally in your browser on your GPU. Nothing you type leaves your machine.
~2 GB one-time downloadDownloaded from Hugging Face (Apache-2.0) only after you confirm, then cached in browser storage (OPFS).
Needs WebGPUDesktop Chrome or Edge with ~2 GB free GPU memory is recommended. Without it, instant retrieval still works.
Grounded — verifyAnswers use only retrieved Atlas passages and cite #numbers. Small models can still err — verify via OpenReview.
ICLR 2027 Atlas — static edition. Scores are preliminary reviewer ratings (1–4) from public OpenReview pages. The Atlas shows scores only and makes no prediction of acceptance. Fact sheets & briefs are AI-written (audited) — verify before citing. Search defaults to keyword and offers optional on-device semantic search via EmbeddingGemma (Built with Gemma; Gemma Terms of Use). Not affiliated with ICLR/OpenReview.
