## Themes & trends

- **Prior-data fitted networks become general-purpose tools.** Theory of non-separable PFN priors (#189), removing up to 96% of TFM depth (#47127), model-aware data preparation (#2906), feature screening with a frozen TFM encoder (#17105), anomaly detection through virtual tasks (#16026), an LLM–table interface for string columns (#22113), and PFN-style transfer to ILP primal prediction (#55619) and zero-shot sequential recommendation (#8253).
- **Generative recommendation over semantic IDs.** Event-level predictive targets (#32242), exposure-ledger routing for item fairness (#32826), hierarchical RL credit assignment for SID tokens (#10298), vertical-domain adaptation (#59680), language-grounded successor dynamics (#13721), and multi-scenario retrieval with hierarchical LMs (#15323).
- **Spending more compute or depth in CTR models without adding parameters.** Loop scaling through recursive layer reuse (#12056) and correlation-regularized depth aggregation (#14935).
- **LLMs over structured data.** Database context compression for Text-to-SQL (#14265), document-to-database construction (#16795), clarification for underspecified spreadsheet edits (#21303), and bridging LLMs with table foundation models (#22113).
- **Finance generative models with market constraints built in.** A binary-token flow-matching foundation model with reward post-training (#18010), arbitrage-free implied-volatility surfaces by construction (#2266), and limit-order-book messages that are replayable by construction (#4454).
- **Conversational systems: memory, simulation and clarification.** Trajectory-aligned user simulation (#8906), search-based memory for role-play agents (#8950), cue-based episode retrieval for dialogue memory (#6570), cost-aware clarification for egocentric assistants (#9138), and question-level credit for medical dialogue (#7185).
