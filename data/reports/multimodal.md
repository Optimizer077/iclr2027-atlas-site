## Themes & trends

- Streaming systems learn when to speak, not only what to say. #3405 couples four assistance decisions, while #4912 jointly trains hierarchical evidence memory and proactive state transitions.
- Spatial supervision moves beyond token agreement: #3146 compares student regions with teacher evidence geometrically, and #49808 derives latent-state supervision from region-masking dependencies.
- Controlled interventions expose hidden failure pathways. #36378 manipulates prerequisite correctness, #38862 changes text beneath fixed driving images, and #23263 traces long-dialogue collapse to cache eviction and repetition.
- Speech work targets temporal failure and faithful synthesis through different mechanisms: #31525 modifies RL and guidance for restoration, whereas #23263 applies inference-time cache and decoding fixes.
- Models increasingly manufacture their own post-training signals. #113 synthesizes verified practice problems, #3146 searches teacher-supported evidence, and #11331 densifies rewards by restructuring trajectories.
