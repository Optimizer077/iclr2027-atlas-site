## Themes & trends

- Generative paths encode process structure: #33584 constrains protein-unfolding geometry, #36990 separates molecular interfaces from fragment realization, and #30908 orders regulatory response before expression.
- Scientific evaluation moves toward physical observables. #47490 tests amorphous materials across experimental and mechanical measurements, while #52923 validates industrial fluid datasets against experiments.
- Single-cell modeling mixes global representation post-training with explicit dynamics: #538 organizes biological semantics once for reuse, #25687 learns Koopman generators, and #30908 factorizes perturbation responses.
- Neural operators use geometry beyond generic equivariance. #20278 removes coordinate shortcuts with invariant boundary features, and #22363 derives spectral operators from axisymmetric Green's functions.
- Representation transfer crosses models and measurement modalities: #28446 combines frozen co-folding representations, whereas #24303 transfers structure-derived chemical information into mass-spectrum embeddings.
