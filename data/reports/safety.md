## Themes & trends

Recurring mechanisms in this selected top-60 set; the pack does not establish change over time or domain-wide prevalence.

- Agent safety evaluation inspects interaction conditions: #50344 supplies controlled environments with verifiable attack outcomes; #48761 tests covert side tasks during successor training.
- Safety depends on how inference routes signals: #37986 evaluates and aligns multiple recurrent depths, while #55334 isolates attention bottlenecks between detection and refusal.
- Privacy guarantees account for pipeline structure: #33858 bounds selection changes in multi-user attribution data; #42970 composes heterogeneous privacy mechanisms end to end.
- Evaluation behavior changes with context: #62247 manipulates test-like formatting, and #35127 varies user-identity cues to measure behavioral shifts.
- Reusable representations support adaptation: #4501 maintains forensic coordinates across changing image conditions; #32835 learns a safety subspace shared across modalities.
