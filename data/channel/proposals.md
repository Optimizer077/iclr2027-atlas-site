# Taxonomy proposals

- [sub:domains-A] theory: Approximation & Expressivity of Neural Networks — universal approximation, minimum width/depth, exact representability and logic-based expressivity for any architecture; split from theory.transformer-theory, which keeps transformer/ICL-specific theory (e.g. #49618, #24979, #27380, #31947, #57895, #22859)
- [sub:domains-A] theory: Learning in Games & Equilibrium Computation — regret dynamics, last-iterate convergence and complexity of equilibria in normal-form, extensive-form and Markov games, where the object is the equilibrium rather than an RL agent; needs a boundary rule against rl.marl (e.g. #39437, #4821, #9346, #17117)
- [sub:domains-A] theory: Abstention, Deferral & Selective Prediction — theory and algorithms for predictors that abstain, reject or defer to experts, including surrogate consistency and abstention–error tradeoffs (e.g. #27211, #22982, #47304)
- [sub:domains-A] optimization: Spectral & Orthogonalized Matrix Optimizers — optimizers acting on the singular-value structure of matrix parameters (Muon, Newton–Schulz, Dion, LMO-based non-Euclidean steps), their theory and kernels; split from optimization.optimizers (e.g. #16449, #20958, #15975, #8176, #28232, #34979)
- [sub:domains-A] optimization: Learning to Optimize & Learned Solver Acceleration — learned models that warm-start, guide or amortize classical LP/NLP/MIP/chance-constrained solvers; the object is the solver pipeline, not a training optimizer (e.g. #46163, #31677, #47599, #14932, #37622)
- [sub:domains-A] optimization: Submodular & Combinatorial Optimization — approximation and oracle algorithms for set functions, matroid constraints and graph subset problems; carves out of optimization.other (e.g. #36186, #48745, #9470, #38247)
- [sub:domains-A] probabilistic: Simulation-Based & Amortized Inference — neural posterior estimation, amortized/prior-conditioned inference and SBI under misspecification, where a trained network replaces per-dataset MCMC/VI; split from probabilistic.bayesian (e.g. #31961, #17926, #10222, #40271)
- [sub:domains-A] probabilistic: Prediction-Powered & Model-Assisted Inference — valid statistical inference combining a small gold-labelled sample with ML/LLM predictions or judges (PPI and variants, judge calibration) (e.g. #32183, #50836, #32656, #48367)
- [sub:domains-A] representation: Test-Time Adaptation — adapting a deployed model with unlabeled test data via statistics, normalization or self-supervised updates, including continual TTA; split from representation.domain (e.g. #30460, #32177, #23113, #23237, #23737, #6601)
- [sub:domains-A] representation: Representation Learning for RL & Control — self-supervised, contrastive and successor-feature representations learned for decision making, and their evaluation; the object is the representation, not the RL algorithm (e.g. #60501, #41395, #26852, #44881)
- [sub:domains-A] representation: Disentanglement, Identifiability & Object-Centric Learning — recovering factorized, identifiable or slot-structured latents from unlabeled data (e.g. #58971, #45315, #48612, #20857)
- [sub:domains-B] evaluation: Interactive Agent Environments — benchmarks where an agent acts in an executable or closed-loop environment (tools, terminals, cyber ranges, simulated users) and is scored on outcomes; split from static Q&A in evaluation.llm-bench (e.g. #10026, #18043, #8406, #13980, #12821, #47546)
- [sub:domains-B] evaluation: Discovery & Open-Problem Benchmarks — benchmarks that score production of new, automatically verifiable results (unsolved problems, mechanisms, compressed proofs) rather than recall of known answers (e.g. #41515, #36733, #61661, #36807, #43241)
- [sub:domains-B] evaluation: Earth, Environment & Energy Datasets — expert- or sensor-collected datasets for ecology, geophysics, emissions and energy prediction (e.g. #53323, #36417, #55219, #56777, #16706, #54080)
- [sub:domains-B] neuro-cog: Spiking Neural Networks & Neuromorphic Computing — SNN neuron models, training, conversion, compression and architectures for efficient computation; split from neuro-cog.brain, which keeps analysis of biological recordings (e.g. #60216, #22725, #38531, #33455, #20021, #13637)
- [sub:domains-B] neuro-cog: Neural Signal Foundation Models — pretraining, adaptation and cross-subject transfer of models on recorded EEG, iEEG or spike data (e.g. #20393, #1076, #776, #41469, #48973, #53344)
- [sub:domains-B] neuro-cog: Task-Trained & Normative Circuit Models — networks trained or optimized on tasks to explain neural dynamics or behavior (RNN solution spaces, cognitive maps, working memory) (e.g. #26891, #43782, #52984, #17819, #34834)
- [sub:domains-B] causal: Treatment-Effect Estimation — estimators and inference for average, conditional, distributional or long-term effects (orthogonal, proximal, IV); split from causal discovery inside causal.causal-inference (e.g. #16716, #41419, #56773, #1631, #43716, #15689, #33583)
- [sub:domains-B] causal: Causal Discovery & Latent Structure — recovering causal graphs or latent causal variables from data, including LLM-assisted discovery; split from effect estimation inside causal.causal-inference (e.g. #61044, #16393, #22243, #22583, #23449, #35270, #61821)
- [sub:domains-B] causal: Program & Controller Verification — specifications, invariants, verified code and certified or repaired controllers; split from theorem proving in causal.formal (e.g. #45074, #41359, #25091, #29383, #26375, #40478)
- [sub:domains-B] applications: Tabular Foundation Models & PFNs — in-context models pretrained on synthetic priors (TabPFN-style), their theory, compression, data preparation and reuse beyond tables (e.g. #189, #47127, #2906, #17105, #16026, #55619, #8253)
- [sub:domains-B] applications: Generative & Sequential Recommendation — next-item and semantic-ID generative recommenders and their training (objectives, RL credit, fairness, adaptation); split from CTR prediction and retrieval in applications.recsys (e.g. #32242, #32826, #59680, #10298, #13721, #8253)
- [sub:domains-B] applications: LLM Interfaces to Structured Data — LLM systems that query or build databases, spreadsheets and tables from language or documents (Text-to-SQL, document-to-database, spreadsheet editing) (e.g. #14265, #16795, #21303, #22113)
